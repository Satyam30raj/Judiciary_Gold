Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you enroll.

Which judiciary exams does Judiciary Gold prepare for?+

We offer complete preparation for 14+ state-wise Judicial Services (PCS-J) and prosecution exams across India, covering Prelims, Mains, and Interview stages.

Can I switch between online and offline classes?+

Yes. All enrolled students can seamlessly switch between live online sessions and offline centre classes based on their schedule and convenience.

Do I get access to recorded lectures?+

Every live class is recorded and added to your dashboard, so you can revise at your own pace, anytime.

Is there a test series included in the course fee?+

Yes, all comprehensive batches include a full Prelims and Mains test series with detailed solutions and rank comparison.

How do doubt-clearing sessions work?+

We host dedicated live doubt sessions each week, plus a direct query system so no doubt is left unresolved before your exam.
